# Setup

:::info[Licensed with the CrZVRC License Manager]
This asset comes with the [License Manager](/docs/documentation/licence). You can add it to your world and try it right away, but you must validate your product license with the License Manager before you can publish your world.
:::

Setting up the Room System in your VRChat world is quick and straightforward. Follow these steps carefully to get everything running.

---

1. **Import the Package:** Ensure you have successfully downloaded and imported the **Room System** package into your project.

2. **(Optional) Import the Notification System:** If you'd like intercom rings and access-denied alerts to also show as on-screen notifications, download and import the separate **Notification System** package too, then locate its prefab and drag a single instance into your active scene hierarchy. This is entirely optional — the Room System works exactly the same without it; you'll just be missing the on-screen alerts. Feel free to skip to step 3 if you don't need this.

3. **Place Your Rooms:** Locate the **Room System** prefab in your project files. Drag and drop this prefab into your scene hierarchy. You need to do this for *every* room you want to create (e.g., if your world has 5 separate rooms, drag the prefab into the scene 5 times and position them accordingly).

4. **Add the Rooms Management:** Next, locate the **Rooms Management** prefab and drag a single instance of it into your scene. This prefab acts as the brain that coordinates all the individual rooms you just placed.

5. **Link the Rooms (and, Optionally, the Notification System) to the Manager:** Select the **Rooms Management** prefab in your scene hierarchy. In the Unity Inspector, look for its Udon script:
   * Drag and drop every single **Room System** prefab from your scene into the rooms array.
   * *(Optional)* If you placed a Notification System prefab in step 2, drag it into the Notification System reference slot. Leave this slot empty if you're not using the integration — the Room System works fine without it, it just won't show the on-screen intercom/deny alerts.
   
> 💡 **Important:** Add the rooms to the array in a logical, sequential order (for example, starting from the first room in a corridor and ending with the last). The system automatically assigns room numbers based on the exact order they are placed in this list.

6. **Open the CrZVRC Tool:** Look at the very top toolbar of your Unity editor. Click on **CrZVRC** and select **Room System** from the dropdown menu.

7. **Generate Render Textures:** A custom editor window will open. Simply click the **Generate Render Textures** button. 

8. **Wait for Confirmation:** The tool will automatically configure the necessary render textures for your UI. Once it finishes, a small popup window will appear notifying you that the setup is complete!
